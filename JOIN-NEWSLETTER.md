# Join the Newsletter

The Muse newsletter is a separate, clearly described subscription. Signup must state the expected cadence, content type, sender identity, and unsubscribe method.

**Suggested copy:**

> Join the Muse newsletter for curated updates, new articles, and occasional hot-item announcements. We respect your inbox and include an unsubscribe link in every issue.
